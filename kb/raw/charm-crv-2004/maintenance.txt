===== Maintenance/Service%20Intervals/Maintenance%20Indicator%20Reset/ 
 
Maintenance Indicator Reset 
[img /images/IMP02Q313/asian150/389671869/] 
 1. Turn off the engine.
 2. Press and hold the Select/Reset button in the instrument panel, then turn the ignition switch ON (Position II).
 3. Hold the button for at least 10 seconds, until the indicator resets.

===== Maintenance/Service%20Intervals/Normal%20Service/110000%20MI%20or%20176000%20KM/ 
 
110000 MI or 176000 KM Inspect 
    Valve Clearance
       Inspect every 110,000 miles, otherwise adjust only if noisy. 
 Replace 
    Spark Plug
    Engine Oil
       Perform at interval or every year, whichever comes first. 
 Rotate 
    Tires
       Rotate Tires. Check tire inflation and condition at least once a month. 

===== Maintenance/Service%20Intervals/Normal%20Service/120000%20MI%20or%20192000%20KM/ 
 
120000 MI or 192000 KM Inspect 
    Steering and Suspension
       Inspect tie-rod ends, steering gear box, boots and suspension components. 
    Parking Brake System
    Fuel Supply Line
       Inspect all fuel lines and connections. 
    Fluids
       Inspect all fluid levels and condition of fluids. 
    Exhaust System
    Drive Belt
    Constant Velocity Joint Boot
    Brakes and Traction Control
       Inspect front and rear brakes. 
    Brake Hose/Line
       Inspect brake hoses and lines (including ABS/VSA if equipped). 
 Replace 
    Oil Filter, Engine
    Fluid - M/T
    Fluid - A/T
       Replace at 120,000 miles, then every 90,000 miles or 5 years. 
    Engine Oil
       Perform at interval or every year, whichever comes first. 
    Coolant
       Replace coolant at 120,000 miles or 120 months, then every 60,000 miles or 60 months thereafter. 
    Cabin Air Filter / Purifier
       Replace dust and pollen filter. If you drive primarily in urban areas that have high concentrations of soot in the air from industry and from diesel-powered vehicles, replace every 15,000miles (24,000 km). 
    Air Filter Element
       Replace air cleaner element. If you drive in dusty conditions, replace every 15,000 miles (24,000 km). 
 Rotate 
    Tires
       Rotate Tires. Check tire inflation and condition at least once a month.
