## Summary
- VIN chars 4–6 `ZC5` = Clarity Plug-In Hybrid (Honda's own statement).
- HV pack max voltage 352 V in this (Canadian 2018) edition. A web-search summary of the US 2018–20 ERG
  quoted "voltage of 348 volts" — I could not open that PDF to confirm (503). Treat max as 348–352 V.
- 12 V battery under the hood; DC-DC converter feeds the 12 V system (separate cut point).
