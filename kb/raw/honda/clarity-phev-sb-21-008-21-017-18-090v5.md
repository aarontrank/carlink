## 21-008 (Feb 11 2021) — verbatim
"Hybrid System Failure Message Comes On with DTC P1D8D. AFFECTED VEHICLES 2018-19 Clarity Plug-In Hybrid ALL ALL; 2020 ALL
JHMZC5***LC000001 thru JHMZC5***LC002657. SYMPTOM The Hybrid System Failure message comes on the MID, and DTC P1D8D
(Generator Motor Inverter Short) is stored. POSSIBLE CAUSE There is an internal failure of the Powertrain Control Unit (PCU).
CORRECTIVE ACTION Replace the PCU." Part "Power Control Unit Assembly (PCU) 1B000-5WJ-A05"; requires draining DT coolant
(new drain-plug O-ring 19012-671-300, Coolant Type 2).
(Note: a web-search summary described this fix as only "drain coolant / tighten plug" — the bulletin itself says replace the PCU.)

## 21-017 (May 28 2021) — verbatim
"Warranty Extension: 2018-21 Clarity Plug-In Hybrid A/C Condenser ... extending the warranty on the A/C condenser to 10 years
from the original date of purchase with unlimited miles. ... The A/C condenser was not manufactured to specification. As a
result, corrosion may develop in the form of tiny holes in the condenser tube walls that allow the refrigerant to leak out."
"CORRECTIVE ACTION Do the inspection procedure and, only if there is a leak in the A/C condenser caused by corrosion or other
manufacturing condition, replace it." Part "Condenser Assembly 80110-TRW-A02". (No evaporator coverage; no DTC.)

## 18-090 v5 (Nov 14 2023) — verbatim
"REVISION SUMMARY Under REPAIR PROCEDURE, Vehicle Emission Recall-Proof of Correction Certificate reorder information was
added." Same background: "DTC P26A3 (electric water valve malfunction), DTC P100C (electric VTC motor speed signal circuit
malfunction) and/or P0010" → "Clear the DTC's and update the PGM-FI software with the latest version."

## Summary
- "Hybrid System Failure" + P1D8D → PCU replacement (covers 2018 VINs entirely).
- A/C condenser corrosion leaks → 10 yr/unlimited-mile extension (2018 cars expire 10 yr from purchase, i.e. ~2027–2028).
- 18-090 is treated as an emissions recall (CA proof-of-correction certificate).
