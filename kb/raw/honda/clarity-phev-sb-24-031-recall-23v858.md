## Verbatim
"Service Bulletin 24-031 May 31, 2024 Version 1 — Safety Recall: 2018–19 Clarity Plug-In Fuel Pump Motor"
"Due to swelling of the fuel pump motor impeller, the fuel pump may seize and stop working."
"Vehicles with eligible VINs and the following symptoms: difficulty starting, engine hesitation while driving, and/or an
illuminated check engine light displayed with DTC P0087, are eligible for immediate repair."
Fuel pressure relief with HDS: "3. Turn the vehicle to ON mode, but do not turn to the READY TO DRIVE mode. ...
6. Enter maintenance mode.
 1. Turn the vehicle to the OFF mode.
 2. Turn the vehicle to the ON mode without stepping on the brake pedal.
 3. With the gear selector in the P position, press the accelerator pedal to the floor twice, then release.
 4. Press the brake pedal and select N mode in the gear selector. Then press the accelerator to the floor twice then release.
 5. Press the brake pedal and select P mode in the gear selector. Then press the accelerator to the floor twice then release.
 6. Apply and hold the brake pedal down. (Do not press the stop/start switch).
 7. Select YES on i-HDS to turn the fuel pump off.
 8. Follow the i-HDS directions and start the engine by pressing the stop/start switch. The engine should be running..."
"Fuel Pressure relief without the i-HDS: 1. Remove the PGM-FI main relay 2. 2. Enter maintenance mode. Start the engine and
let it idle until it stalls."
"Disconnect the 12-volt battery negative terminal. ... Disconnect and isolate the cable with the 12-volt battery sensor from
the 12-volt battery."
"36. Use i-HDS with the DST-i to do an all DTC check ... 37. Do the steering angle neutral position relearn procedure."

## Summary
- Recall symptom DTC: P0087 (fuel rail/system pressure too low).
- Honda's dealer "maintenance mode" (pedal sequence above, from ON-not-READY) forces the engine to run while parked —
  the documented way to run the engine on demand (e.g., for emissions/readiness checks). This is distinct from the
  automatic "engine started because it hasn't run for a long time" behaviour in the owner's manual.
- Repair involves opening the fuel tank unit (access cover, seals); post-recall EVAP DTCs (P0455/P0456) and "Emission
  System Problem" are reported by owners (see community file), not by Honda.
