## dead-12-volt-battery-experience.11454 (Jun–Jul 2021) — verbatim
- Landshark, Jun 30 2021: "If the car is ON/READY, the 12V battery will be charged by the Lithium battery via the DC/DC converter/charger."
- Mad_River, Jun 30 2021: "HV Charge mode has nothing to do with charging the 12V battery."
- vicw, Jul 1 2021: leaving the car on in EV mode "will apply about 14.55 V continuously to your 12 V battery."; with headlights
  on AUTO but not lit, it "will drop back in around 90 seconds to a 12.6 V charge rate."; during a plug-in session "13.3 V applied
  to the 12 V battery".
- MrFixit, Jul 1 2021: the 12 V is charged "albeit at a lower voltage, 13.3" while the HV battery charges.
- neal adkins, Jun 30 2021: 12 V "went dead after charging cell phone and playing radio for about 25 minutes in accessory mode";
  failed "The very next day After the 3 year mark".

## battery-charging-module.19808 (Sep–Nov 2024) — verbatim
- StickWare, Sep 18 2024 (2018, 184k mi): "Plug in Charging System Problem - Range Limited"; would not charge on three chargers; new 12 V
  did not fix; dealer diagnosed the charger ("$5100 for it plus installation").
- JoeN, Nov 22 2024 (2018, 86k mi): "They fixed it under the CA Emissions Warranty."; DTCs "P0D27 and P0D5C"; installed "battery charger
  1C600-5WJ-A11" and "coolant type-2 OL999-9011".
- Landshark, Sep 22 2024: charger "covered for 15 years or 150,000 miles in Oregon and 9 other states" (emissions warranty), 8 yr/100k elsewhere.
- MrFixit, Sep 21 2024: inspect charge-port pins for damage/recession before replacing the module.

## Summary
12 V is charged only via the IPU DC-DC converter: ~14.5 V in READY, ~13.3 V while plug-in charging, nothing in ACC/ON.
Replacement group 51R (positive post right). On-board charger failure → "Plug-in Charging System Problem. Range Limited" with
P0D27 (+P0D5C) → charger replacement (1C600-5WJ-A11), often under emissions warranty.
